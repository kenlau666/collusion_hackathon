## The precise contribution and strongest objections

The proposed causal contrast holds physical state, task obligations, source action traces, current reward information and surviving actors fixed, changing only inherited public plan exposure. All original actors/private access are stopped in every branch. The estimand is the exposure-induced change in actual reward-only cycles and completed care, then how that effect changes under reward correction, care-first instruction or a pending-care reserve rule. Log proposed, denied and executed actions separately. The unrestricted exposed/neutral comparison identifies exposure; mechanically impossible cycles under a gate do not identify a behavioral mechanism.

A one-misaligned-plus-honest reference and a fixed-state last-required-kit diagnostic constrain whether the result requires intentional coalition members or partner-contingent decisions. Ordinary independent bonus maximization can yield a resource round trip. Even contingent responses can be rational planning. Do not infer shared malicious intent, covert communication or a new cognitive mechanism from those observations.

The native joint score itself rewards the cycle. Intended-purpose harm needs independent, predeclared care outcomes under native-equivalent turns, not the cycle credit or a regret label. The four-tool scripted deficit is constructed; actual agents may finish care first or use batched tools. Bonus zero with transfers still legal is the strongest simple baseline. A care-first instruction also leaves execution possible. A reserve rule must preserve useful transfers in both directions, use explicit feasible obligations and acknowledge that cycles can remain possible after obligations are satisfied. Its mechanism is established task-aware authority restriction.

The inspected sources do not establish the exact combined causal/utility comparison. That is a bounded search conclusion, not proof of literature-wide absence. The skeptical review reached lower-edge 8 only after these discriminators were specified; earlier reviews remain 7–8.
